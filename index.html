SanDisk · Customer experience · 2025–2026

Made self-service work in 40+ markets.

  • 30% lower handle time
  • 20% higher automated containment
  • 45% fewer manual handoffs

The problem

Customers were giving up on self-service and ending up with agents, but nobody knew where or how often. A $2.5M IVR and chatbot programme needed a build order, not a wish list.

What I did

Mapped 15+ user journeys and sized every drop-off by volume. Owned the roadmap and requirements with engineering and an external vendor. Traced the Genesys to Oracle Service Cloud data flow to find where data moved through people instead of systems, and drove adoption across regional teams without formal authority.

The result

Handle time fell 30%, automated containment rose 20%, and manual handoffs dropped 45%. The KPIs and dashboards I defined decided what each following wave would ship.

Western Digital · Operations · 2023–2025

Found a bottleneck nobody had raised.

  • 40% faster processing
  • 238 SKUs automated
  • 27 releases owned

The problem

Product-catalog updates relied on a manual extraction and reconciliation step. It was slow and error-prone, and no one had flagged it.

What I did

Put a cost on it, built the business case for API-based sync, and after leadership sign-off led it from problem definition through UAT to deployment. Owned launch quality across 27 releases and 9,000+ test cases.

The result

Processing time fell 40% across 238 SKUs, the manual error class disappeared, and every acceptance criterion was met before go-live.

CARS24 · Machine learning · 2022

Caught listing errors before buyers did.

  • +20% model accuracy (mAP)
  • 2,000+ vehicles analysed
  • Live in production

The problem

Odometer readings on vehicle listings didn’t always match reality, which eroded trust on a live consumer marketplace.

What I did

Analysed listing data for 2,000+ vehicles to find the error patterns, helped define the problem for automated odometer reading, and worked with product and engineering to build validation into the production pipeline.

The result

A computer-vision model shipped into the live marketplace, with accuracy (mAP) up 20% through iterative testing.

How I built it

The pain. Job seekers check dozens of careers pages, wade through stale reposts, and still apply after hundreds of others.

The product. It reads official careers pages of 118 companies (50 in India, 68 abroad that hire from India), keeps roles within ±6 months of your experience, ranks the freshest first, and includes jobs abroad only when the posting offers relocation or visa support.

What I owned. Product definition, a landing page and two-step onboarding with every step measured, an admin dashboard with cookie-free analytics, separate dev and prod environments with versioned releases, and the security and privacy work for the private beta.
